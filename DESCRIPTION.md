# Healthie — description

**GitHub about (short)**  
Scan a pack. Get a 0–100 score in plain English — food, body & beauty, pet. Independent. No brand pays for a better number.

**Store / press (one breath)**  
Healthie reads the pack in front of you. Barcode or photo. You get a score you can say at the shelf, the teaspoons of sugar, the additives worth watching, and a better neighbour in the same aisle. No brand pays for a better number. Not a diagnosis.

**What it is**  
A consumer reading aid for three aisles: food, body & beauty, pet food.

**What it is not**  
Not a medical device. Not a paid ranking. Not Yuka with a new coat. Not Open Food Facts dumped on a phone. Not a social network.

**Promise**  
Independent scores. No brand pays for a better number.

**Proof on the pack page**  
0–100 · Excellent / Good / Poor / Avoid · why, in sentences · ingredients named · swap in the same aisle.

**Share card**  
Cream paper, Healthie H, “Scan a pack. See the score.” 1200×630 JPEG. See [docs/OG.md](docs/OG.md).

**Audience**  
Shoppers who want the aisle to make sense in ten seconds. Parents, pet people, anyone tired of marketing on the front and chemistry on the back.

**Disclaimer (always available)**  
Healthie is a reading aid, not a diagnosis. If you have an allergy, always check the pack.
