# Brand

Healthie looks like cream paper and a firm number. Not a wellness influencer. Not a lab.

## Name

**Healthie** — one word, capital H. Never Healtie, never HEALTHIE in UI, never HealthIE.

## Promise

Independent scores. No brand pays for a better number.

## Lockup

`public/brand/healthie-logo.png` — mark + word. Use the mark alone only where the word already appears nearby (app icon).

## Surface

- Paper cream background (`#F1F0EB` territory)
- Ink for type
- Score ring is the loudest object on a product page
- No neon, no glassmorphism, no fake 3D food

## Voice

See [MESSAGING.md](MESSAGING.md). Same voice in the app (`src/lib/copy.ts`), this repo, and store listings.

## Independence

Do not sell placement. Do not let a brand badge sit next to the score. Sponsored anything is out of scope forever.

## Houses

Healthie is a Digital Currensy Inc. house. It does not merge with EventFix, AutoShield, CLIP OR NOT, or a music OS.
