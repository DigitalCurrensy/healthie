Healthie is a pack reader with an honest mixer. 0–100. Food, body & beauty, pet. No brand pays.

## Earned the hard way

- Camera in an embedded preview is a browser lock, not a decoder bug. Stop rewriting ZXing for that.
- Doritos must not read Good. UPF + thin nutrition + flavour enhancers cap below 50.
- Nutri-Score letter is not the shop headline. The 0–100 disc is.
- iMessage grey chip on `*.workers.dev` is the host, not missing tags. Never demo that URL.
- `Vary: User-Agent` + a crawler stub fights Apple TN3156 and can serve the stub to humans.
- `|preview|` in a bot regex matches Grok-Preview and replaces the app with an image.
- Share tags live in `scripts/grok-pwa-shared.mjs`. Identity lives in `src/lib/og/site.json`.
- Pack photos must belong to the GTIN.

## Honest gaps

- Unique photography still thin on world-pantry long tail.
